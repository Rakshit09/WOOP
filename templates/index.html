👋 How to WOOP?

Why WOOP?

WOOP helps with project resourcing, planning, and tracking effort vs. revenue for projects.

The WOOP Protocol

📅
Monday: Forecast

Think future! What do you plan to do this week? Fill this on Monday.

✅
Friday: Actuals

Look back! What did you actually do? Confirm your week on Friday.

The WOOP Board

Heat Map

This is a visual snapshot of your activity. Top Row = Plans = Forecast, Bottom Row = Reality = Actuals.
Click any box to jump to that week.

Breakdown Chart

This shows the percentage (%) of time you have spent on specific projects to date.
Hover over the slices to know more.

The WOOP Rules

Open - The current week. It's ready for you to fill in.
Completed - Great! You've submitted this entry.
Missing - Oops! A past week actual entry was missed. Please go back and fill it.
Locked ->Future dates are locked. Old Forecasts expire if missed. So get it green on time.

"Get 'em all Green!" ✨
